We're in network with most major PPO plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and Aetna. Send us your card before your visit and we'll confirm your coverage and give you a written estimate.

Your first visit takes about an hour: a 3D scan, low-dose X-rays, a full examination and a conversation about your goals. You leave with a written plan and costs for anything we recommend.

Yes. We keep two emergency slots open every weekday. Call before 11:00 and we'll see you the same day for pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a lost filling.

Tell us when you book. We'll schedule a longer appointment, explain each step before it happens and agree on a stop signal. Headphones, weighted blankets and gentle sedation are available.
